# Decision log: 2026-11-18, Account email

Decided with the project owner on 2026-11-18, for
[../web-accounts.md](../web-accounts.md) section 8:

- Every account has an email address, used for its first sign-in,
  password resets and account notices.
- A new account receives a one-time set-password link, never a
  password.
- Mail is sent through an SMTP relay configured in the environment.
- Accounts without an address add and verify one at their next
  sign-in.
- Notices sent: the security ones (password, address, new device,
  disabled, role). Others may be added later.

Settled while the mockup was adjusted:

- Opening the set-password link verifies the address, since the link
  was delivered to it.
- An account changes its own address in its settings (current password
  required, verification link to the new address, notice to the old).
  An admin cannot change another account's address, except to correct
  it while the account is still invited (never signed in). A
  superadmin can change any account's address, for an account that
  lost its mailbox; the change is audited and the old address is told.
